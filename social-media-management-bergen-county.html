FAQ

Questions we hear most often.

Which social platforms should my business be on?

Usually two or three, not all five. Most local businesses do best on Instagram and Facebook, with TikTok or YouTube Shorts for video and LinkedIn for B2B. We pick based on where your customers actually spend time.

How often should a local business post?

Consistency matters more than volume. Most local businesses do well with three to five posts a week on their main platform. We set the exact number in your plan.

Do you reply to comments and messages?

Yes. Community management is included, and anything that needs your answer, like a quote request, gets sent straight to you.

Can you use photos and videos we already have?

Yes, as a starting point. Original footage from your business performs better than stock or recycled content, so most plans include regular shoots.

How much does social media management cost in Bergen County?

At AAG, social plus content production starts at $3,500 a month. Posting-only plans cost less. You get a fixed price after the free audit.

How do you measure results?

Reach and followers, but mainly the actions that pay the bills: profile clicks, calls, messages, and form fills. You get a report every month.
