Free advice

Five things you can do this week

No agency needed for these. They're the basics we'd fix first anyway.

  1. 01

    Update your Google profile

    Correct hours (holiday hours too), a menu link, and ten fresh photos of food and the room.

  2. 02

    Reply to every review

    Good or bad, within a few days. Keep it short, thank them, and take complaints offline.

  3. 03

    Film one dish

    Vertical, under 20 seconds, from prep to plate. Post it as a Reel.

  4. 04

    Give your slowest night a reason

    A special, an event, or a pairing. Post it two days ahead, not the same night.

  5. 05

    Ask regulars to tag you

    A table card or a word from the server. Their posts reach people who trust them.

FAQ

Questions we hear most often.

What kind of social media content works for restaurants?

Short vertical video: food being made, plated, and served, plus the people behind it. It outperforms still photos on Instagram and TikTok.

Do restaurant ads work?

Yes, when they're local and specific: a few miles around the restaurant, built around a reason to come in this week, like a special, an event, or a slow night.

How often should a restaurant post?

Several times a week on Instagram, plus stories most days. One monthly shoot can produce most of a month's posts.

Does Google Business Profile matter for restaurants?

It's often the first thing people see. Fresh photos, accurate hours, and replies to reviews all affect whether they pick you.

How much does restaurant marketing cost?

It depends on how often we shoot and post, and whether you want ads. You get a fixed monthly price after the free audit.

Do you work with bars, cafes, and caterers too?

Yes. Any food or hospitality business that sells on how it looks and feels.
