Free checklist

Is your ad account set up to work?

Run through this before you spend another dollar. If two or more are a no, that's usually where the money is leaking.

  1. 01

    Conversion tracking is on

    You can see which calls and form fills came from ads, not just clicks.

  2. 02

    Ads go to the right page

    Each ad sends people to a page about that service, not your homepage.

  3. 03

    Campaigns are split by service

    Separate campaigns for your main services, so you can see what each one costs per lead.

  4. 04

    Negative keywords are in place

    Searches like "jobs," "free," or "DIY" are blocked so you don't pay for them.

  5. 05

    Location targeting is tight

    Ads reach people in your area, not people who are only "interested in" it from somewhere else.

  6. 06

    Someone checks the searches weekly

    The search terms report gets reviewed and cleaned up every week.

FAQ

Questions we hear most often.

How much should a local business spend on Google Ads?

Enough for the campaign to learn. For most local service businesses, we suggest starting around $1,000 a month in ad spend, then scaling what produces leads at a cost you're happy with.

Google Ads or Facebook ads: which is better?

They do different jobs. Google catches people already searching for your service. Facebook and Instagram reach people before they search. Most clients start with whichever matches how their customers buy, then add the other.

Who owns the ad accounts?

You do. We build and manage everything in your own accounts, so you keep the data and history.

How fast do ads produce leads?

Usually within the first few weeks. The first month is about collecting data. Months two and three are about lowering your cost per lead.

Do you build landing pages?

Yes. A dedicated page with one clear offer converts far better than sending ad traffic to a homepage.

What do your reports show?

Spend, leads, cost per lead, and which campaigns and keywords produced them, plus what we're changing next month.

We tried ads and they didn't work. Why would this be different?

Usually the problem is the landing page or the tracking, not the platform. We fix both before spending another dollar, so you can see exactly what works.

How much does ads management cost?

Our management fee is separate from your ad spend, which Google or Meta bill directly to your own account. You get a fixed monthly fee after the free audit.

How long do I have to commit?

Terms are flexible. Every client is different, so we agree on a length that fits your business and your goals.
